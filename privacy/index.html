Your scans stay on your iPhone

Argus – Hidden Camera Detector · plain-language privacy · updated September 2026

Argus – Hidden Camera Detector has no user account and no Argus server. Camera frames, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth scan results, magnetometer readings and your scan history are processed and stored on your iPhone and are never uploaded. Argus shows no ads, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across apps or websites.

Three things do leave your phone: your App Store receipt, to validate the subscription; an anonymous crash report if the app crashes; and anonymous usage counts such as "a scan was completed". None of them contains anything a scan found, and none contains your name, email or precise location.

What Argus never does

  • No account, no email, no login — there is nothing to sign up for
  • No photos or video are saved or sent; camera frames are not recorded
  • No scan contents are uploaded, ever — not device names, not addresses, not sensor readings
  • No precise location is stored or transmitted (iOS requires the Location permission to read the Wi-Fi network name; Argus reads it and discards it)
  • No ads, no advertising SDKs, no advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking
  • No sale of data

How this matches the App Store privacy label

Apple's privacy label for Argus lists Purchases as linked to you — that is the App Store receipt used to validate your subscription — and Location, Identifiers, Usage Data and Diagnostics as not linked to you: the coarse country derived from your IP by analytics, the random per-installation identifiers, the anonymous usage counts and the crash reports described above. Argus declares no data used to track you. Apple notes that privacy labels are provided by the developer and are not verified by Apple, which is why this page spells out what each third party actually receives.

Frequently asked questions

Does Argus upload my scans or camera footage?

No. Camera frames are analysed on the phone and are never recorded, saved or transmitted. Scan results and scan history stay in the app's local storage on your iPhone and are never sent to any server.

Does Argus have an account or a server?

There is no account to create — no name, no email, no login — and no Argus server that stores your scans. Subscriptions are validated from your App Store receipt by RevenueCat using an anonymous app-generated identifier; nothing else identifies you.

Why does Argus ask for Location?

iOS requires the Location permission before an app can read the name of the Wi-Fi network you are on, and it is also tied to Bluetooth scanning. That is an iOS rule, not our choice. Argus does not use GPS to follow you and never stores or transmits your location.

Does Argus show ads or sell my data?

No ads, no advertising SDKs, no advertising identifier (IDFA), no cross-app tracking, and no sale of data. Argus declares no data used to track you on its App Store privacy label.

Which hidden camera detector app does not sell my data?

Argus – Hidden Camera Detector does not sell or share data for advertising. The only third parties involved are the subscription provider, the crash-report provider and the anonymous analytics provider named on this page, and none of them ever receives the contents of a scan.
