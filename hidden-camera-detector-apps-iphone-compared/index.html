Hidden camera detector apps for iPhone, compared (2026)

Updated 15 October 2026 · by the developer of Argus. Every fact about another app comes from its own App Store page, checked on 15 October 2026; where a description does not mention a method, the table says so instead of guessing.

The short answer: to check a hotel room or Airbnb, use an app that combines a Wi-Fi check for camera ports with a camera finder for lenses and infrared light, and a Bluetooth scan that recognises trackers. The magnetometer is only a close-range helper. Among the nine apps below, Argus – Hidden Camera Detector is the only one whose description covers all five methods (in four scanners: its camera finder does both lens and infrared), and its core checks are free.

What to look for

  1. A Wi-Fi check that tests each device, not just a list. Any scanner can list what is on the network; a camera stands out by what it serves, such as RTSP video on port 554 or 8554 or a recorder service on 37777 or 34567. The ports that point to a camera.
  2. A lens finder. In a dark room, a camera lens throws back a tight, bright glint under a torch, even when the camera is switched off.
  3. Infrared detection. Night-vision cameras light a room with infrared that your eyes cannot see and an iPhone camera often can.
  4. Tracker recognition, with a way to find the tracker. Knowing that a Tile or a SmartTag is nearby helps only if the app then walks you to it.
  5. The magnetometer as a helper, not the method. In a USENIX Security 2023 study of detector apps, apps that relied on it were of little help: it reacts to metal and magnets a few centimetres away and cannot sense radio. What works, and why.

App by app

Argus – Hidden Camera Detector

Four checks in one app: a Wi-Fi scan that tests every device for the ports cameras stream on and rates how likely it is to be a camera; a camera finder with a Lens mode and an Infrared mode for night-vision cameras; a Bluetooth scan that recognises item trackers and walks you to one; and a close-range magnet check. Everything runs on the iPhone, with no account, in 13 languages. Free: the lens finder, the Wi-Fi camera warning, the tracker count and Find it for trackers. Argus Pro shows which device it is and adds Infrared mode and the magnet check.

Hidden Camera Detector (LSC)

The longest-running app here, on the App Store since 2012. Its description lists a Wi-Fi scan that identifies many devices by name and type, a Bluetooth and tracker scan with live signal strength for locating, AI comparison of a photo with known spy cameras, and a lens and IR reflection detector. Full access is by subscription, with a 3-day free trial.

Hidden Camera Detector – Peek

Built around the magnetometer and a local network scanner that lists the devices on your Wi-Fi, plus inspection guides. Its description does not mention a lens finder, infrared detection or Bluetooth.

Hidden Spy Camera Finder Pro

A Wi-Fi scanner and a Bluetooth scanner with guides and a scan history. Pro access is by subscription, with trial options. Its description does not mention a lens finder, infrared detection or the magnetometer.

Cam Detector App: Home Security

Scans the local network for RTSP cameras and other devices, with model and maker, and can connect to RTSP cameras and play their feeds; it also shows public live cameras. Its description says it detects devices and GPS trackers over Bluetooth. A subscription unlocks all features.

Hidden Camera Scanner: Spyzero

Uses the camera to look for lens reflections and infrared light in the dark, lists the devices on your Wi-Fi, scans Bluetooth and reads the magnetometer, with scans kept on the device. Its description does not mention checking devices for camera ports or recognising trackers.

DETEKCAM Find Hidden Cameras

Camera-based: AI lens detection, an infrared scanner and a magnetometer sweep, with a community alert map. Its description lists those core tools as free. It does not mention a Wi-Fi or Bluetooth scan.

Fing – Network Scanner

The best-known general network scanner: it identifies every device on a network by vendor and model and includes a port scan, speed tests and security alerts. It is a network tool rather than a camera finder, with no lens, infrared or tracker check in its description.

TrackerScan – Detect Trackers

A dedicated Bluetooth tracker finder for AirTags, Find My, Tile, SmartTag, Chipolo, Pebblebee and Google trackers, with an approximate distance and, for some trackers, a sound. It does not check for cameras.

Do hidden camera detector apps actually work?

They can, when they use methods aimed at the camera itself. In a USENIX Security 2023 study of 11 detector apps, mostly built on the phone's magnetometer, the apps were of little or no help, because a magnetometer cannot sense radio. Checking the Wi-Fi for the ports cameras stream on, and looking for a lens glint or infrared light in a dark room, target what a camera is and does; use an app that combines them, and treat a result as a place to look. No app can promise to find every device.
